Managing your membership

Choose when to stop renewing.

huddle+ and huddle* renew until cancelled. If you subscribed through Apple or Google, manage cancellation in that store’s subscription settings. Check your renewal date and the store’s terms before making a change.

The app shows current prices in your store’s currency before you purchase. Paid benefit sharing is separate from Family pet linking. Care bookings depend on local availability.
