About

Built for the pets
that are yours —
and the ones who aren't yet.

Every feature, every line of code, every late-night fix — built for the moments your pet stopped being "a pet" and became one of you. And for the strays, the fosters, the runaways, and the in-betweens who deserve the same kind of love.

Our mission
No pet left behind.

To build the most trusted local pet network in the world — where urgent help arrives in seconds, every carer is known and verified before you hire them, and every pet has a digital home for a lifetime of records.

Safety, community, and care, in one place. Identity-verified by default. Local-first by design.

Why we exist

Every second
counts when a
pet goes missing.

A lost dog or injured stray can vanish into a neighbourhood within minutes. Social media is noisy, unverified, and doesn't know where you are.

huddle is a real-time, location-aware, identity-verified network that activates the right people — your actual neighbours — in the moment they're needed most.

But emergencies are only the start. Pet life is also about everyday community and trusted care. So we built huddle to serve all of it: urgent situations, local connections, care booking, and pet records — together, in one app.

What we stand for

Five principles.
One purpose.

01

Safety is urgent

Pet emergencies don't wait for you to find the right group. huddle treats urgency as a design requirement — real-time, location-precise, delivered to verified neighbours first.

02

Identity builds trust

Every member completes identity verification. No anonymous accounts. This isn't bureaucracy — it's the foundation that makes strangers trustworthy enough to hand your pet to.

03

Local means something

Your neighbourhood is not a postal code. It's the dog-walker you wave at, the rescue volunteer two streets away, the forum where your specific block gets real answers.

04

One app, not many

Pet owners juggle group chats, social pages, separate booking apps, paper vaccination records. huddle brings it all into one place, because coherence is care.

05

Pet people, all kinds

huddle supports Dogs, Cats, Birds, Fish, Reptiles, Small Mammals, Farm Animals, and more — and every kind of pet person: owners, fosters, rescue volunteers, shelters, vets, and trusted carers. If you care about animals, you belong here.

How it grew

From a single
missing dog to a
whole network.

The beginning

The problem became personal

A pet went missing. The owner posted everywhere — every group chat, every social feed they could think of — and waited hours for anything useful. Meanwhile, a neighbour who had seen the animal walked past without ever knowing anyone was looking. huddle was born from that exact gap.

Listening

Talking to pet people

We sat with owners, rescue volunteers, fosters, groomers, dog walkers, and shelter staff. The same themes surfaced over and over: fragmentation, verification anxiety, the exhaustion of managing care across a dozen platforms.

Building

Safety first. Community second. Care third.

We sequenced huddle deliberately. Real-time safety features (Live Map, Broadcast Alerts) came first — because they had to work before anything else mattered. Then the social fabric (Forum, Discover, Groups, Chats). Then trusted care booking and pet records. Each layer reinforces the others.

Today

Built for pet people everywhere

huddle is a native iOS and Android app. We're growing city by city — because local trust is the only foundation that scales. The team works close to the streets the product is built for, and close to the NGOs and shelters doing the hardest work.
