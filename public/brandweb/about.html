About

Built for the pets
that are yours —
and the ones who aren't yet.

huddle is a global pet app for people who want to feel closer to others who care about animals. You can come here to make friends, or to ask for help when a pet needs someone.

Our mission
No pet left behind.

We want more pets to have people looking out for them. huddle gives pet people a place to meet and stay in touch, with tools for the care and concerns that come with loving an animal.

You can join huddle with a pet, or simply because you care about them.

Why we exist

Every second
counts when a
pet goes missing.

When a pet is missing, a clear photo and a recent sighting can help people understand what to look for.

The huddle Map gives a pet alert a place people can return to for updates. Sharing the link lets others follow the same report.

There is room for everyday pet life here as well. A group walk or a helpful conversation can make caring for a pet feel less lonely.

What we stand for

What matters to us

01

Safety is urgent

Pet alerts should be easy to understand and share. We encourage members to keep their reports up to date.

02

Identity builds trust

Identity checks help you understand a profile. Getting to know someone and asking about their experience matters too.

03

Local means something

A familiar face can make a walk more enjoyable. huddle helps you find people you might like to meet again.

04

Keep useful details together

A pet profile helps you keep care notes handy when someone needs to know your pet’s routine.

05

Pet people, all kinds

huddle supports Dogs, Cats, Birds, Fish, Reptiles, Small Mammals, Farm Animals, and more — and every kind of pet person: owners, fosters, rescue volunteers, shelters, vets, and trusted carers. If you care about animals, you belong here.

Why we are building huddle

The beginning

Finding help for a pet

Someone who sees a missing pet may not know anyone is looking. A shared alert can help connect that sighting with the person searching.

Listening

Feeling at home with pet people

It feels good to talk with someone who understands life with a pet. huddle makes space for those conversations and for friendships that can grow from them.

Building

Making care arrangements clearer

Care lets a pet owner and carer discuss what is needed before agreeing to a booking. Clear expectations help both people prepare.

Today

Built for pet people everywhere

huddle is a global pet app for iPhone and Android. You can also browse public content on the web.
