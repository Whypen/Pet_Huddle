How trust gets built

Get to know
who will care for your pet.

Ask about the care your pet needs, especially anything that may be new to the carer. Their profile can help you start the conversation; the agreement should reflect what you both decide.

1

They verify, first.

Look for the identity badge and read the profile. Identity checks do not establish experience with your pet’s needs.

2

They show up, in public.

Read what the carer chooses to share on their profile. A conversation gives you a chance to ask about anything that matters to your pet.

3

They earn it, review by review.

Read booking reviews for details that matter to you, such as communication and experience with a similar pet. Ask the carer about anything you are unsure of.

⚕ Licensed Medical Care

Check professional credentials before booking.

If a service requires a professional licence, review the provider’s credentials and check with the relevant licensing body. An identity badge alone does not confirm that someone is qualified to provide medical care.
