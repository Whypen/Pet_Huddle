Getting started with huddle

Help with your account and first steps

How do I sign in to huddle?

Use email and a password, or choose an available Apple or Google sign-in option. If you forget your password, follow the reset link on the sign-in screen. You can review extra account security options in Settings.

How do pet care apps verify a provider's real identity?

The identity badge shows that an account has completed huddle’s identity checks. It does not tell you how experienced someone is with your pet’s needs. Read the carer’s profile and ask about relevant experience before agreeing to a booking. Learn about choosing a carer.

Can I join a pet community app before I get a pet?

You are welcome on huddle even if you do not have a pet. It is a place to meet people who enjoy being around animals and want to help them. You can join the conversation while you are thinking about your first pet, too.

What is the difference between a Wave and a Star on huddle?

A Wave lets you say hello in Discover. A Star is another way to start a conversation, subject to account eligibility and your available balance. Free has no monthly Stars included; huddle+ includes 4 and huddle* includes 10. Check your balance in the app before sending one.

What are a Care Scope and a Start PIN?

The Care Scope records the arrangements you and the carer agree to before a booking. The Start PIN is used at handoff to confirm the start of care. Follow the booking instructions and only share the PIN when you are ready for the session to begin.

What is the exact broadcast reach and duration by plan?

Broadcast limits are up to 5 km and 12 hours on Free, 10 km and 24 hours on huddle+, and 20 km and 48 hours on huddle*. Super Broadcast offers up to 50 km and 72 hours where available. Choose the reach and duration in the app. These are limits, not a promise that everyone in the area will receive or read the alert.

Is there a pet app that supports rabbits, birds, and reptiles, not just dogs and cats?

huddle supports pets beyond dogs and cats, including birds, reptiles and small mammals such as rabbits. Choose the pet type when creating a profile and add care notes for your animal. Available carers will depend on your area and the species they work with.

What pet care services can I book online — walking, boarding, grooming, training?

Care includes categories for sitting and visits, boarding, grooming, training, rescue or foster support, pet transport and licensed medical care. The services you can book depend on local availability. Ask about the provider’s qualifications when your pet needs specialist care.

Do pet care booking apps charge a service fee?

Paid Care bookings can include service charges for the pet owner and the carer. Review the booking’s fee breakdown and total before confirming. If you are offering care, check the payout shown for your booking. The booking terms explain payment and cancellation rules. Read how huddle Care works before arranging a booking.

Making friends through pets

How do I make friends as an adult through pets?

Choose a pet activity you would enjoy doing again, such as a short walk with someone whose dog suits yours. A shared routine gives you another chance to talk without having to plan a big occasion.

You can browse public groups or get to know someone on huddle before suggesting a meeting. Try a specific invitation: “Would you and your dog like company for a short walk this weekend?” Agree on a public meeting place and let a friend know your plans.

If you get along, suggest another walk. That guide includes research on time spent together and friendship.

Can having a pet help me make friends?

A pet can give you something easy to talk about with someone new. Ask about their dog’s favourite walk, or join a conversation about a pet you both love.

A 2015 survey by Lisa Wood and colleagues found that pet owners were more likely to know people in their neighbourhood. Some reported friendships and support through people they met because of their pet. This was an observational study, so it cannot prove that getting a pet causes friendship. Read The Pet Factor, published in PLOS ONE.

On huddle, you can browse public conversations before deciding to join. You are welcome even if you do not have a pet.

How do I turn a dog walking acquaintance into a friend?

If you enjoyed a walk together, suggest another one with a time they can say yes to. You could say, “I enjoyed the company today. Would you fancy another walk on Saturday morning?” Let them choose what feels comfortable.

Jeffrey Hall’s research found that time spent together and shared leisure activities were associated with closer friendships. It does not give everyone a fixed number of hours to become friends. Read How many hours does it take to make a friend?

A regular group can give you a reason to see familiar people again. Browse public groups on huddle to see what interests you. For a first meeting, choose a public place and agree on a walk that suits both pets.

How can I meet pet people if I feel shy?

Start with a conversation you would actually enjoy. You might ask someone how they keep their cat entertained indoors, or reply to a question you know the answer to. There is no rush to arrange a meetup.

Research by Gillian Sandstrom and Elizabeth Dunn found that everyday contact with acquaintances was associated with feelings of belonging and well-being. The study did not test huddle or show that every conversation improves how someone feels. Read Social Interactions and Well-Being.

You can read public pet conversations first and join when you feel ready. If you would prefer a shared activity, look for a pet group that fits your interests.

Lost pets, alerts, and emergencies

How can I avoid a lost pet scam?

It is natural to want to believe someone who says they have found your pet. Ask for proof before acting, and keep one identifying detail out of your public report so you can ask about it later.

Never share a login or verification code. If someone claims to be a vet or shelter, find the organisation’s official number yourself and call to check. A photo alone can be faked. Do not send a reward or urgent payment before confirming they have your pet.

PawBoost’s lost-pet scam guidance explains these warning signs. Save suspicious messages and report the account on huddle if the contact happened here.

How can I alert people nearby if my pet goes missing?

Open the Map and create a Lost Pet alert with a recent photo and the last known location. Choose the available broadcast reach, then share the alert link with people who can help. Notification delivery depends on nearby members’ settings and device permissions. See how Broadcast Alerts work.

Is there a map for reporting lost pets, strays, or hazards near me?

Yes. The huddle Map lets you share a pet alert with a reported location. Add useful details and a photo if you have one. People reading can return to the report for updates.

What are the pin types on the huddle Live Map?

The Map has Lost, Stray, Caution and Others pins. Choose the one that fits your report so someone browsing can understand it quickly. See how pet alerts work.

What should I do if I lose my pet?

Start looking nearby and contact animal services or shelters with a recent photo. Let your vet know, and check that your microchip registration has your current contact details.

On huddle, add a Lost Pet alert with where and when your pet was last seen. Include a clear photo and anything that will help people recognise them. Share the alert link and update it when you have news. This shelter’s lost-pet guidance also recommends checking shelters and sharing a clear report. App notifications alone cannot reach everyone who might help.

If someone says they have your pet, check their claim before sending money or personal details.

How do I report a stray or injured animal near me?

Add a Stray alert with what you saw and when you saw it. A clear photo can help someone recognise the animal. If it appears injured or needs urgent help, contact an appropriate animal rescue service or vet directly.

How do I report a dead animal on the road?

Contact the appropriate animal services or road authority to report it. You can add an Others pin on huddle if it may help someone identify a missing pet. Keep any description respectful and avoid sharing graphic images.

How do I warn nearby users about poison, hazards, or a dangerous area?

Use a Caution pin to describe the hazard and where you saw it. Stick to what you observed; if you are unsure what a substance is, say so. Contact the appropriate authority when the situation needs a direct response.

Can I use huddle to warn people about a suspected pet thief or dangerous person?

You can report a safety concern, but keep the description factual and avoid naming or accusing someone without evidence. Do not post private personal details. Contact the appropriate emergency service if someone is in immediate danger.

How do I edit or remove a pin on the huddle Live Map?

Open your own pin on the Map and look for its editing or removal options. Update the report when circumstances change so people helping do not act on old information.
