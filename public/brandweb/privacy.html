What personal information Huddle collects, why Huddle uses it, when Huddle shares it, how long Huddle keeps it, how Huddle transfers it, and what rights and choices users may have.

Effective date: 20 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information Huddle collects, why Huddle uses it, when Huddle shares it, how long Huddle keeps it, how Huddle transfers it, and what rights and choices users may have.

1. Scope

This Policy applies to Huddle's app, websites, legal pages, support channels, marketplace tools, social features, map features, payment flows, identity verification features, and related care features.

2. Information Huddle collects

Huddle may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account and authentication information, including email address, password, date of birth, and sign-in method (including Apple ID tokens where Apple Sign-In is used).
  • Profile and pet information, including display name, photos, social handle, location, bio, preferences, and pet details.
  • Identity verification information, where a user completes identity verification: legal name, card brand and last four digits submitted through our payment partner for card checks, and liveness or human-presence data captured through the device camera. Camera-based liveness data is processed only to confirm a real person is present and to help prevent fraud. Huddle does not store raw facial recognition templates or biometric identifiers after verification is complete.
  • Security settings information, including two-factor authentication enrolment status and, where biometric authentication is enabled, a device-level credential reference. Biometric data (Face ID, Touch ID) is stored and processed only on the user's device and is never transmitted to Huddle.
  • Content and communications, including messages, posts, comments, reviews, support requests, and booking details.
  • Payment and transaction information, including subscription status, billing events, refund and dispute records, and limited payment metadata.
  • Device, app, and usage information, including device type, operating system, app version, push notification token, IP address, crash data, and usage data.
  • Location information, where enabled.
  • Trust and safety information, including phone-verification status, card-check status, fraud signals, and moderation records.
  • Information from third parties, such as app stores, payment partners, analytics providers, and other users where relevant.

3. How Huddle uses information

Huddle uses information to create and secure accounts; support sign-in methods including Apple Sign-In and biometric authentication; provide messaging, discovery, map, care, social, and subscription features; verify identity for trust and safety; process payments; personalise notifications and experience; improve the service; send allowed communications; comply with law; enforce terms; and protect users, pets, Huddle, and the public.

4. Sharing, visibility, and retention

Huddle may share information with vendors, service providers, other users where a feature requires it, payment and app-store partners, analytics or measurement partners, legal authorities, or parties involved in business transactions. Huddle keeps information as long as reasonably needed for service, business, legal, fraud-prevention, dispute, and safety purposes.

5. Location, automation, and choices

If location access is enabled, Huddle may use location data for discovery, maps, alerts, care features, friend location pins, fraud prevention, and analytics. Huddle may use automated tools for ranking, recommendations, spam control, moderation, and platform security. Depending on law, users may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, receive, withdraw consent for, or opt out of certain uses of personal information. To exercise these rights, see Privacy Choices.
