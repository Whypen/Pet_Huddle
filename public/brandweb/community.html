Groups

Make the next walk
a group plan.

If you keep meeting the same people at the park, a huddle Group gives you a place to arrange the next visit. Create an event with a meeting place and time so members can RSVP. Ask the organiser what to expect if it is your first visit.

Public Groups for finding new regulars. Private Groups for the trusted circle — photo dumps, holiday cover, the emergency vet group chat.

✓ Explore groups by neighbourhood or shared interest
✓ Check the joining rules before requesting a place
