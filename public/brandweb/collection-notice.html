Personal Information Collection Notice

This notice summarises the personal information HUDDLE collects when you use its services and why it is needed. Read it together with the Privacy Policy and Privacy Choices.

1. Information HUDDLE may collect

HUDDLE may collect account, profile, pet, communications, location, device, payment, verification, care-booking, safety, support, and usage information. The exact information depends on the features you choose to use.

2. Why HUDDLE collects it

HUDDLE uses this information to provide and secure accounts and features, support social, discovery, map, and care services, process payments and payouts, prevent fraud and abuse, handle reports and disputes, communicate with you, and meet legal obligations.

3. When information is required

Some information is needed to create or secure an account, use a requested feature, complete verification, process payment, or provide a Care Scope. If you do not provide required information, that feature may not be available. Optional information is clearly identified where possible.

4. Who may receive it

HUDDLE may share information with the other user when a feature requires it, with infrastructure, payment, mapping, notification, support, analytics, and verification providers, and with authorities or other parties where required for safety, law, or a corporate transaction.

5. Your choices and requests

You can manage many controls in the app. For access, correction, deletion, objection, portability, consent withdrawal, or other privacy requests, see Privacy Choices.
