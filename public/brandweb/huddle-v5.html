⚕ Licensed Medical Care

We verify the vets too. Properly.

For Licensed Medical Care providers, huddle matches credentials against external registries, certificate databases, professional organisations, and licensed directories. A Verified badge means we found the match. When we can't auto-match — sometimes registries are private, paper-only, or sit in a different jurisdiction — we flag it so you can do a final check before booking.

Most apps don't even try. We try, and tell you what we find.
