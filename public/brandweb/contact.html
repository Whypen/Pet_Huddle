How we work

Close to the city.
Close to the
pets.

The huddle team works close to the streets the app is built for — close to the parks, the clinics, the shelters, and the everyday neighbourhoods where pet life happens.

We actively look for ways to collaborate with NGOs, shelters, rescue organisations, and pet-welfare groups — at no cost to them. Our role is to provide tools, reach, and trusted community so the people already doing the hardest work can do more of it.

huddle never asks anyone for funding. We earn support by providing real value — to pet people, carers, partners — in exchange for the chance to keep serving every pet.

For NGO or partnership conversations, email enquiries@huddle.pet.
