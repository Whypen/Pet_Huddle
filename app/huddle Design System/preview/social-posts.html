Editorial cleanliness · vertical-first
Torn paper × Huddle Blue × uppercase display
Illustration style — "The Friendly Outliner." Every character, pet, and key visual is drawn in flat monoline vector: uniform 2.25pt stroke, rounded caps & joins, 90° rotational simplicity. No sketch marks, no texture, no shading, no gradients, no 3D, no photography of illustrated subjects.
  • Ink stays ink. Outlines are solid #0E0E10, never tinted.
  • One flat pop color per illustration (Blue, Coral, or Lime — never combined), offset 2–4px from the stroke so it peeks, 20–40% coverage.
  • Characters are friendly, slightly rounded, not chibi. Dot eyes, simple smile curve, 4–5 head-to-body proportions for humans. Pets match the bear mark's warmth.
  • Human skin is always white. Faces, hands, and limbs are left unfilled — never tinted with the pop color or any skin tone.
  • Phone & device KVs use the same 2.25pt monoline — drawn, not rendered. Screen content is live huddle UI composited inside the outlined frame.
  • No background. Illustrations ship on transparent canvas. The surface shows through — never bake a paper or blue backdrop into the illustration itself.
  • Never do: AI-generated art, clipart, stock vectors, emoji in place of illustration, outline-only on blue, multiple pop colors per scene.
