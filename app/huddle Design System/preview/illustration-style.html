The Friendly Outliner · coloring rules
One flat pop color, intentionally offset from the line.
  • Ink stays ink. All outlines are solid #0E0E10 — never tinted.
  • Fills are flat. No gradients, no shading, no soft brush texture. Solid color only.
  • One pop color per scene. Default is Huddle Blue. Coral or Lime may substitute, never combine.
  • Offset the fill 2–4px from the stroke on one axis so it peeks out — screen-printed, not colored-in.
  • Coverage 20–40%. Fill one or two shapes (a sweater, a collar) — leave the rest as ink-only silhouette.
  • No background. Illustrations sit on transparent canvas. Let the surface — white, blue, torn paper — show through. Never ship an illustration with a baked-in backdrop.
  • Human skin is always white. Leave it unfilled so the surface reads as skin. Never tint faces, hands, or limbs with the pop color.
