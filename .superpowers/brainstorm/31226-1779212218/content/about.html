Why we exist

Every second
counts when a
pet goes missing.

In Hong Kong's dense urban neighbourhoods, a lost dog or injured stray can disappear into thousands of households within minutes. Traditional social media groups are noisy, unverified, and don't know your location.

huddle was created to solve that. A real-time, location-aware, identity-verified network that activates the right people — your actual neighbours — in the moment they're needed most.

But we didn't stop at emergencies. Pet ownership is also about community, care, and daily life. So we built huddle to serve all of it: urgent situations, trusted local connections, everyday care booking, and organised pet records — in one place.

Mission & Vision
Our mission
No pet left behind.

To build the world's most trusted local network for pet people — where urgent help arrives in seconds, carers are known and verified before you hire them, and every pet has a digital home for their lifetime of records.

Vision
A world where no pet suffers alone.

We believe that the right information, reaching the right people, at the right moment can save a life. We're building toward a future where every neighbourhood has an active, verified pet-people network — and no animal is left without help.

Our approach
Local first.
Trust first.

Every huddle member completes identity verification. Every carer on the platform has a community presence before you book them. Safety features use your actual location — not a generic city radius. Community starts with your actual neighbours.

What we stand for

Four principles.
One purpose.

01

Safety is urgent

Pet emergencies don't wait for you to find the right Facebook group. huddle treats urgency as a design requirement — real-time, location-precise, and delivered to verified neighbours first.

02

Identity builds trust

Every member on huddle completes identity verification. No anonymous accounts. This isn't bureaucracy — it's the foundation that makes strangers trustworthy enough to hand your pet to.

03

Local means something

Your neighbourhood is not a postal code. It's the dog-walker you wave at, the rescue volunteer two blocks away, the forum where your specific street gets real answers. huddle is built around that.

04

One app, not many

Pet owners already manage too many fragmented tools: WhatsApp groups, Facebook pages, separate booking apps, paper vaccination records. huddle brings it all into one place because coherence is care.

05

Pet people, all kinds

huddle supports dogs, cats, rabbits, hamsters, birds, reptiles and more — and every kind of pet person: owners, fosters, rescue volunteers, shelters, vets, and trusted carers. If you care about animals, you belong here.

Our journey

From idea to
neighbourhood.

The beginning

The problem became personal

A pet went missing in a dense Hong Kong neighbourhood. The owner posted everywhere — WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram — and waited hours for any useful response. Meanwhile, the right neighbour who had seen the animal walked past without ever knowing anyone was looking. huddle was born from that gap.

Research

Listening to pet people

We interviewed owners, rescue volunteers, fosters, groomers, dog walkers, and shelter staff across Hong Kong. The same themes surfaced repeatedly: fragmentation, verification anxiety, the exhausting inefficiency of managing pet care across a dozen separate platforms.

Build

Safety first, community second, care third

We sequenced huddle deliberately. Real-time safety features (Live Map, Broadcast Alerts) came first — because they had to work before anything else mattered. Then community (Social Forum, Discover, Groups, Chats). Then trusted care booking and pet records. Each layer reinforces the others.

Now

Built in Hong Kong, for pet people everywhere

huddle is a native iOS and Android app, built by a team that lives in the same dense, multilingual, pet-loving city as its first users. We're growing locally first — because local trust is the only foundation that scales.
