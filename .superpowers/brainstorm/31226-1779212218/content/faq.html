Getting started

What is huddle?

huddle is a pet safety, community and care app built in Hong Kong. It brings together four things into one native iOS and Android app:

Live Map & Broadcast Alerts for real-time pet safety. Social Forum, Discover, Groups, Chats for a local pet community. Care for verified local pet services. Pet Profiles for organised pet records. Every member completes identity verification.

Is huddle free?

Yes. The core safety, community, and pet record features are free forever — including Live Map, 5km Broadcast Alerts, Social Forum, Discover, Groups, Chats, Pet Profiles, and access to the Care marketplace.

huddle Plus and huddle Gold are paid plans that add wider broadcast reach, more Stars, advanced filters, profile boosts, and Family Link. See Plans & Pricing for details.

Where is huddle available?

huddle was built in Hong Kong and launched in Hong Kong first, where the team lives. The app works anywhere — but the verified-neighbour density and local Care marketplace are strongest in Hong Kong today. Other cities follow as the verified-member network grows.

What age do I need to be?

huddle is for users aged 13 and above. Discover and stranger chat are 16+ only. Some features have additional gating.

Safety — Live Map & Broadcast Alerts

How does huddle Live Map work?

Tap a location, pick a pin type (Lost Pet, Stray Animal, Caution, or Others), add a photo and description, and drop the pin. The pin becomes visible on the Live Map to nearby members and is anchored to the real coordinates.

How far does a Broadcast Alert reach?

Broadcast Alert reach depends on your plan: Free → 5km, huddle Plus → 10km, huddle Gold → 20km, Super Broadcast (add-on) → 50km. Alerts push as a real-time notification to verified members within that radius.

Can I edit or remove a pin after dropping it?

Yes — you own your own pins. Edit photos, update the description, change the pin type, or remove the pin entirely as the situation evolves. Found your pet? Mark it resolved with a tap.

Will Broadcast Alerts wake people up at night?

Notification timing follows iOS / Android system rules and your recipients' notification settings. Members can adjust which categories of alerts they receive and during which hours.

Care marketplace

How are huddle Care providers verified?

Every provider on huddle Care has completed identity verification — government ID, liveness check, phone confirmation. No anonymous providers, ever. Many providers also have visible community presence in the Social Forum, Groups, or Discover before you book them.

What's the service fee?

A 10% service fee is split across both sides of the booking. There are no hidden surcharges — what you see at checkout is what gets paid.

How are disputes handled?

If something goes wrong with a booking, huddle's structured dispute process reviews booking records, in-app messages, photos, and reviews. We resolve fairly and may hold funds during review. See the Terms of Service for details.

Can I book the same carer outside the app?

We don't recommend it. Bookings outside huddle lose payment protection, dispute support, verified-identity context, and review history. Keep bookings inside the app — that's how trust accumulates for everyone.

Community

What are Stars?

huddle Stars are how you reach out to a huddle member you don't already know — a deliberate, intentional gesture instead of a flood of cold messages. Every plan includes Stars: Free → free allowance, Plus → 4/month, Gold → 10/month. They protect your inbox from spam outreach.

What's the difference between Discover and Social Forum?

Discover surfaces individual pet people in your area — for connection, meetups, mutual follows. Social Forum is for local discussion: questions, advice, rescue threads, recommendations. Different tools, different intents.

Public Groups vs Private Groups?

Public Groups are discoverable by any huddle member in your area — good for neighbourhood walks, breed circles, rescue networks. Private Groups are invite-only — for trusted circles where you share photos, plan meetups, and coordinate care.

Plans, payments, family

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open huddle, go to Settings → Subscription, tap Cancel. If you purchased through the App Store or Google Play, manage the subscription through that purchase channel per their rules. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

What is Family Link?

Family Link lets you extend select huddle Plus or Gold benefits to one trusted relative (except Stars, which are personal). Available as part of Plus and Gold, or as a $4.99/month add-on on Free.

Can I get a refund?

Subscriptions are generally non-refundable once the paid period or feature begins, except where required by law or by the App Store / Google Play purchase channel's rules. See the Terms of Service.

Privacy & trust

What does huddle do with my location?

Location is used for nearby discovery, map participation, alerts, safety features, service availability, fraud prevention, and analytics — only with your permission. You control location access in the app and at the device level. See the Privacy Policy.

What does identity verification actually check?

A government-issued ID check, a liveness check (confirming you're a real person), and phone verification. huddle does not intentionally store raw biometric identifiers — the liveness check is used only to confirm a real human and reduce abuse.

Who can see my profile?

Depends on your settings and which features you use. Profile visibility, Discover participation, Forum posts, Group memberships, and Care listings are each independently configurable.
