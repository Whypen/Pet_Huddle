Pin types

Four pins.
Real situations.

huddle Live Map uses four pin types — each tuned for a different urgent local situation. Pins include a photo, a description, and a precise location.

Lost Pet

Your pet is missing. Drop a pin where they were last seen — verified neighbours within your chosen reach are alerted immediately.

Stray Animal

You've spotted an unfamiliar pet that may be lost or in need. Report the location so the right people can respond.

Caution

Hazards that pet people should know about — aggressive animal, unsafe location, suspicious behaviour, environmental risk.

Others

Anything urgent that doesn't fit the categories above. Useful for unusual situations that still need local visibility.
